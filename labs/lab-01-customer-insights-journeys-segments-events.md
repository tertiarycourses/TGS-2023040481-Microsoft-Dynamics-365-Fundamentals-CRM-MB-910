# Lab 1 - Customer Insights Journeys, Segments, Forms, and Events

## Objective

Describe how Dynamics 365 Customer Insights - Journeys supports lead generation, segments, marketing forms, email marketing, customer journeys, events, and Copilot scenarios.

## Scenario

Contoso Home Services wants to promote a new annual maintenance package. The marketing team needs to capture interest, segment customers, send personalized emails, and invite qualified prospects to a webinar.

## Prerequisites

- Review Customer Insights - Journeys documentation.
- If available, open a Dynamics 365 Customer Insights - Journeys trial or trainer tenant.

| Segment | Criteria | Message angle |

### Step 4 - Outline a customer journey

1. Customer submits form.
2. Customer receives confirmation email.
3. If email is opened, send webinar invitation.
4. If webinar is attended, create qualified lead.
5. If no response after seven days, send reminder.

Add the decision points and actions to a flow diagram or bullet list.
