# Lab 3 - Lead to Opportunity Process and Guided Selling

## Objective

Describe Dynamics 365 Sales lead qualification, opportunity management, guided selling, and business process flows.

## Scenario

The marketing team sends qualified leads to sales. Sales representatives need a consistent way to qualify leads, track opportunities, and guide next steps.

## Prerequisites

- Review Dynamics 365 Sales lead and opportunity concepts.
- If available, open Dynamics 365 Sales in a trial or trainer tenant.

### Step 2 - Define qualification criteria

Create a qualification checklist:

- Need confirmed
- Budget identified
- Decision maker known
- Timeline understood
- Product or service fit confirmed
- Consent and communication preference recorded

### Step 3 - Map the lead to opportunity process

Draw or describe:

1. New lead is created.
2. Sales representative contacts lead.
3. Lead is qualified.
4. Account and contact are created or matched.
5. Opportunity is created.
6. Sales activities are tracked.
7. Opportunity is won or lost.

### Step 4 - Design opportunity stages

Create stages for:

- Qualify
- Develop
- Propose
- Close

For each stage, define required activities and exit criteria.

### Step 5 - Identify guided selling features

List how these help sales users:

- Business process flows
- Suggested activities
- Sales accelerator
- Relationship insights
- Copilot summaries
