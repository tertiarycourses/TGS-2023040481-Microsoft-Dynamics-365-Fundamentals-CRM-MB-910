# Lab 4 - Product Catalog, Quotes, Orders, Invoices, Forecasting, and Copilot

## Objective

Describe product catalog concepts, price lists, quotes, orders, invoices, sales pipeline, forecasting, Sales Insights, and Copilot in Sales.

## Scenario

Contoso sells maintenance packages with optional add-ons. Sales managers need consistent pricing, quote tracking, pipeline visibility, and forecasting.

## Prerequisites

- Review Dynamics 365 Sales product catalog and quote lifecycle.
- Review forecasting and Copilot for Sales concepts.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Design a simple product catalog

Create a catalog table:

| Product | Unit group | Base unit | Price list | List price |
|---------|------------|-----------|------------|------------|

Add:

- Basic maintenance package
- Premium maintenance package
- Emergency service add-on
- Annual inspection

### Step 2 - Map the quote lifecycle

Describe the lifecycle:

1. Opportunity requires proposal.
2. Quote is created from opportunity.
3. Products are added.
4. Discount or price adjustment is reviewed.
5. Quote is activated.
6. Customer accepts or rejects.
7. Accepted quote becomes order.
8. Order supports invoicing.

### Step 3 - Define sales pipeline fields

Create a pipeline table:

| Opportunity | Stage | Estimated revenue | Close date | Probability | Owner |
|-------------|-------|-------------------|------------|-------------|-------|

Add at least five sample opportunities.

### Step 4 - Explain forecasting

Write short notes for:

- Forecast period
- Forecast category
- Pipeline amount
- Committed amount
- Best case amount
- Sales manager review

### Step 5 - Identify Copilot and related app scenarios

List use cases for:

- Microsoft 365 Copilot for Sales
- Copilot opportunity summary
- Email drafting
- Meeting preparation
- LinkedIn Sales Insights
- Sales mobile app

## Deliverable

Submit:

- Product catalog table
- Quote lifecycle map
- Sales pipeline table
- Forecasting notes
- Copilot and related app use cases
