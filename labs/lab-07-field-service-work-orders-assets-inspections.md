# Lab 7 - Work Orders, Assets, Inspections, and Mobile Field Service

## Objective

Describe Dynamics 365 Field Service work order lifecycle, inspections, mobile app, customer assets, and asset management.

## Scenario

Contoso sends technicians to customer homes for inspections and repairs. Dispatchers need a clear work order process, technicians need mobile instructions, and customer assets must be tracked.

## Prerequisites

- Review Dynamics 365 Field Service work order concepts.
- Review inspections, customer assets, and mobile app concepts.
