# Lab 8 - Resource Scheduling, Connected Field Service, and Field Service Copilot

## Objective

Describe resource management, scheduling, Connected Field Service, and Copilot scenarios in Dynamics 365 Field Service.

## Scenario

Contoso dispatchers schedule technicians manually. Some technicians lack the right skills for assigned jobs, and preventive maintenance is not triggered automatically from device signals.

## Prerequisites

- Review resource scheduling in Field Service.
- Review Connected Field Service concepts.
- Review Copilot in Field Service concepts.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Define resources

Create a resource table:

| Resource | Type | Skills | Territory | Working hours |
|----------|------|--------|-----------|---------------|

Include:

- Technician
- Crew
- Equipment
- Facility

### Step 2 - Define scheduling requirements

For a work order, define:

- Required skill
- Duration
- Location
- Time window
- Priority
- Required parts
- Customer preference

### Step 3 - Compare scheduling options

Create a comparison table:

| Scheduling method | Best for | Benefit |
|-------------------|----------|---------|

Include:

- Manual scheduling
- Schedule board
- Schedule assistant
- Resource scheduling optimization

### Step 4 - Describe Connected Field Service

Create an IoT scenario:

1. Customer asset sends sensor alert.
2. Alert creates a case or work order.
3. Dispatcher reviews issue.
4. Technician is scheduled.
5. Repair is completed.
6. Asset status is updated.

Identify the business value of proactive service.

### Step 5 - Identify Copilot scenarios

List how Copilot can assist:

- Summarize work order history
- Draft customer update
- Help dispatcher understand scheduling conflicts
- Help technician find relevant service instructions

## Deliverable

Submit:

- Resource table
- Scheduling requirement list
- Scheduling method comparison
- Connected Field Service scenario
- Copilot use case list

## Checkpoint questions

1. Why are skills and territories important for scheduling?
2. What is the difference between schedule assistant and optimization?
3. How does Connected Field Service change the support model?
