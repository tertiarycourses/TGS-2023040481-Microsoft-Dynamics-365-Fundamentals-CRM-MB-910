## No tenant? Get a trial

- **Dynamics 365 free trial (30 days)** — <https://trials.dynamics.com>. Choose the app you want to try (Sales, Customer Service, Field Service or Customer Insights); each trial provisions a Dataverse environment with sample data.
- **Microsoft 365 Developer Program** — <https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/dev-program> for a renewable development tenant.
- **Power Apps Developer Plan** — <https://powerapps.microsoft.com/developerplan/> for a free Dataverse environment (Power Platform features only; Dynamics 365 apps are not included).

## Features that may need a licence

| Feature | Lab | If you don't have it |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Insights – Data | 4 | Use the guided demo environment, or follow the documentation walkthrough and record the pipeline stages |
| Sales Insights premium | 6 | Review the settings page and the Microsoft licensing documentation; complete the comparison table from it |
| LinkedIn Sales Navigator | 6 | Review the Microsoft documentation on the Lead and Account controls and record the capabilities |
| Omnichannel for Customer Service | 8 | Use the Customer Service workspace and the documentation for the channel and supervisor features |
| Resource Scheduling Optimization | 10 | Review the documentation; the manual and schedule-assistant methods work in any trial |
| Connected Field Service / Azure IoT | 10 | Create the IoT Device and IoT Alert records manually, as the lab directs |
