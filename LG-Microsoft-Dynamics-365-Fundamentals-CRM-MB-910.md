## Microsoft exam status

Microsoft Learn states that Exam MB-910 was retired on **December 31, 2025**. This learner guide keeps the published MB-910 customer engagement objectives as a structured way to learn Dynamics 365 CRM concepts, customer journey scenarios, sales processes, service operations, field service workflows, and shared platform capabilities.

## Course goal

This learner guide helps you understand how Dynamics 365 customer engagement apps support marketing, customer data, sales, service, field operations, analytics, collaboration, and productivity. The labs emphasize business process understanding rather than deep customization.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the labs, you should be able to:

1. Describe Dynamics 365 Customer Insights - Journeys and its role in campaigns, forms, segments, events, and customer journeys.
2. Describe Dynamics 365 Customer Insights - Data and its role in unified customer profiles, measures, segments, and predictions.
3. Explain Dynamics 365 Sales lead, opportunity, quote, order, and invoice processes.
4. Describe product catalogs, price lists, guided selling, forecasting, sales insights, and Copilot for Sales.
5. Explain Dynamics 365 Customer Service cases, queues, routing, SLAs, entitlements, knowledge, omnichannel, and agent productivity tools.
6. Explain Dynamics 365 Field Service work orders, inspections, mobile app usage, resource scheduling, assets, and Connected Field Service.
7. Describe Microsoft Dataverse, Power Platform, activities, views, charts, dashboards, and reporting in customer engagement apps.
8. Describe Microsoft Teams, Outlook, Excel, Word, and SharePoint integration with Dynamics 365.
9. Identify practical Copilot scenarios across customer engagement apps.
10. Build a complete customer engagement process map from marketing to sales to service and field operations.

## Case study used throughout the labs

You are a junior business applications consultant for **Contoso Home Services**, a company that sells home maintenance packages, runs marketing campaigns, manages sales opportunities, supports customers through service cases, and sends technicians to customer sites.

Contoso wants to use Dynamics 365 customer engagement apps to:

- Capture leads from marketing forms and events.
- Segment customers based on interests and service history.
- Qualify leads and manage opportunities.
- Generate quotes and track sales pipeline.
- Support customers through cases, SLAs, queues, and knowledge articles.
- Schedule technicians for field work.
- Track customer assets and inspections.
- Use dashboards, reports, Teams, Outlook, Excel, Word, SharePoint, and Copilot to improve productivity.

## How to complete each lab

1. Read the scenario and objective.
2. Open the listed Microsoft references.
3. If a Dynamics 365 environment is available, navigate to the relevant app and observe the feature.
4. If no tenant is available, complete the lab using Microsoft Learn screenshots, documentation, and the scenario tables.
5. Record the business purpose of each feature.
6. Answer the checkpoint questions.
7. Save the deliverable before moving to the next lab.

## Exam and learning guidance

Because MB-910 is retired, use the objectives as platform-learning guidance. Focus on:

- What each customer engagement app is for.
- Which business processes each app supports.
- How Dataverse underpins Dynamics 365 apps.
- How Microsoft 365 and Power Platform extend CRM scenarios.
- Where Copilot can assist users in marketing, sales, service, and field work.

## Final capstone expectation

At the end of Lab 10, you should have a complete customer engagement map that shows:

- Marketing journey from lead capture to segmentation.
- Customer profile and unified data use.
- Sales lifecycle from lead to invoice.
- Service lifecycle from case to resolution.
- Field service lifecycle from work order to completion.
- Shared platform capabilities across apps.
- Collaboration and productivity integrations.
- Copilot use cases and learner readiness notes.
